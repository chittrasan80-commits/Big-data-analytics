What the retailer should do

Champions

Protect the revenue base with loyalty rewards and early access. Fewer than one in five customers bring in about 69% of sales.

Potential Loyalists

Bought recently but only about three times. Bundles, repeat-purchase offers and reminders can move them toward Champions.

Lost / Hibernating

The biggest group (42%) brings only 7% of revenue. Use low-cost win-back emails, not heavy discounts.

UCI Online Retail: 541,909 transactions from a UK-based online gift retailer, December 2010 to December 2011. After removing guest checkouts, cancellations and invalid rows, 4,338 identified customers remain.
